SubPulse 💓
​The Heartbeat of Subscriptions

License: MIT

Vibe Coded
​SubPulse keeps a constant pulse on your recurring expenses. It’s an automated micro-SaaS dashboard designed to track, organize, and manage all your software subscriptions, memberships, and recurring costs in one unified place—sending proactive email notifications before you get charged.

​✨ Features
​Real-Time Subscription Pulse: Single dashboard to view active recurring spend, monthly burn rate, and annual projections.
​Proactive Renewal Alerts: Timely email notifications delivered before upcoming renewal dates so you never miss a cancellation window or surprise auto-debit.
​Smart Categorization & Insights: Group expenses by category (AI, Work, Media, Utilities) and payment methods to identify hidden software inflation.
​Custom Billing Schedules: Support for monthly, quarterly, annual, and custom recurring payment cycles.
​Modern, Responsive UI: Built for rapid navigation and effortless tracking across desktop and mobile devices.
